Our services

Cleaning built around how you actually live and work

Pick a recurring plan to keep things effortlessly maintained, or book a one-time reset when you need a deeper level of clean.

Recurring Home Cleaning

Your regular reset. Kitchens and bathrooms sanitised, floors vacuumed and mopped, surfaces dusted, beds made and trash taken out — on a weekly, biweekly or monthly rhythm.

  • Same crew whenever possible
  • Kitchen & bathroom deep attention
  • Floors, dusting, trash, tidy-down
Most requested

One-Time Deep Clean

The full top-to-bottom. Baseboards, door frames, vents, inside appliances, grout lines, light fixtures and all the places a routine clean skips. Ideal before guests arrive or after a long Phoenix dust season.

  • Inside oven, fridge & cabinets on request
  • Baseboards, vents & light fixtures
  • Shower grout & hard-water treatment

Office & Commercial

Dependable janitorial service for offices, clinics, salons, studios and retail spaces. Scheduled after hours or before open so your team never works around us.

  • After-hours & weekend availability
  • Restrooms, breakrooms, common areas
  • Consumables restocked on request

Move-In / Move-Out

Empty-property cleaning for tenants, homeowners and property managers. We clean inside every cabinet, closet and appliance so the place hands over inspection-ready.

  • Inside all cabinets & closets
  • Appliance interiors degreased
  • Landlord & realtor friendly

Post-Construction Clean-Up

Fine construction dust gets everywhere. We do a staged clean — debris, then dust removal, then a finish polish — so a finished remodel actually looks finished.

  • Multi-pass dust removal
  • Window, track & sill detailing
  • Paint fleck & adhesive removal

Add-On Detail Work

Build your clean up. Interior windows, patio sliders, refrigerator interiors, oven interiors, laundry fold-down and garage sweep-outs can all be added to any visit.

  • Interior windows & sliders
  • Fridge / oven interiors
  • Patio & garage sweep-out
